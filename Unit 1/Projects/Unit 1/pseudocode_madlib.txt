Pseudocode - MadLib

In this document, you will practice writing some pseudocode.

What is pseudocode?
It is a plain-English, human-readable description of a computer program's logic or algorithm

Why do we do it?
1. It helps us plan what we want to do! It is a draft in non-code
2. It helps us focus on the logic and steps we hope to accomplish
3. Others can read your pseudocode and get a good gist of what you are aiming at
4. You can do this practice for any coding language!

Example
Aim: I want my program to add or subtract numbers based on user inputs
Needs: variables, if/then options, casting (string to int), print statements, inputs
print: welcome to...
number1: enter a number (input)
number2: enter another number (input)
operation: would you like to add or subtract? (input)
IF operation is add then:
	number1 + number2
ELSE:
	number1 - number2
print: your answer is...

MadLib Pseudocode
Now is your chance to try some pseudocode!
Requirements:
1. Need at least 10 fill-in-the-blanks; they can nouns, proper nouns, amount, adjectives, adverbs
2. You need a sample story that you are going to place your blanks within
3. Need to be able to fill in the blanks with your inputs
